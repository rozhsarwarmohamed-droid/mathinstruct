Reading Analogue Clocks

Students look at a traditional clock face and write the correct time. Tip: the short fat hand = hours, the long thin hand = minutes.

Hour hand → 2  |  Minute hand → 1
02:05
Hour hand → near 10  |  Minute hand → 9
09:45

🖊️

Drawing Clock Hands

Students are given a digital time and must draw the short hour hand and long minute hand in the correct positions on an empty clock face.

Given time → draw the hands
09:45
Minute hand → pointing at 9
Hour hand → close to 10
Your turn — check after!
03:15
Minute hand → pointing at 3
Hour hand → just past 3

🔗

Matching Times

Students draw lines to connect each analogue clock face with the correct digital time.

Draw a line from each digital time to the matching clock face:
07:20
11:55
03:10
03:10
07:20
11:55

🌟 Quick Tips

  • For analogue clocks, remind them: the short hand tells us the hour, and the long hand tells us the minutes.
  • When writing digital time, always use a zero for single-digit hours — so “five past nine” is 09:05, not 9:05.
